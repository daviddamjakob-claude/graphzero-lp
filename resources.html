03 — What we believe

Taking the AI Leap is a high stakes strategy and change management effort.

That needs a technology foundation that is robust, secure and sovereign.

01

AI applications on their own can only reap a small margin of potential benefits while exposing organizations to uncontrollable risks.

02

It needs a dedicated deployment team, ideally with direct mission by the CEO, to find use cases, design solutions and own the implementation into the organization.

03

One-to-one connections to source systems are not sufficient when moving a company towards AI-enabled working. It needs a knowledge base as an additional context layer between source systems and agentic applications to make AI applications really powerful while keeping your knowledge sovereign.

04 — Who we work with (ICP)

In-house teams and integrators use our technology to build their AI transformation agenda on a robust, secure and sovereign infrastructure.

01

02

Priority integrators

Assumption that a significant part of these companies works with integrators / technical consultancies. Focus at least initially on channel partners with <50 employees where we can quickly talk to a decision maker and a design partnership can be initiated quickly.

In-house teams
